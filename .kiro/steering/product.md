# Product: TrialReach

Finding the research deserts of Massachusetts. Hackachusetts 2026, Healthcare & Biotech, challenge H5 (Making clinical research more accessible).

Shows counties where many people have a disease but few recruiting clinical trials are nearby, so hospital research offices, trial sponsors and state health officials know where to open the next study site and who to reach.

Full requirements: docs/01-PRD.md. Read docs only when a task needs them.

## Rules
- Score places, never people. No personal health data.
- Never say a person qualifies for a trial. Not medical advice.
- Always cite sources: ClinicalTrials.gov, CDC PLACES, MassGIS.
- Plain, simple language on screen.
